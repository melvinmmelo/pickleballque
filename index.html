Open Sideout

Fair open play on every court.

Sideout runs your pickleball queue. Everyone can see who’s on court and who’s next, and one tap starts the next game. Free, no accounts, on any phone, tablet or gym TV.

On deck

Three taps from check-in to game point.

Sideout keeps one line for all your courts, so nobody has to remember who was next.

  1. 1

    Add players

    Type nicknames as people arrive, or put up a QR code and let players check themselves in from their phones.

  2. 2

    Start games

    Each court fills with the next four in line. Add as many courts as your venue has; they all share the same queue.

  3. 3

    Finish and rotate

    One tap counts the game, sends those four to the back, and calls the next four. Game counts keep the night fair.

    Everything an open-play night needs.

    • One queue, every court

      First in, first out across all your courts. Skip a player for one game without losing their spot.

    • Custom lineups

      Send a chosen foursome to the back together. Each player gets two per session, so it stays fair.

    • Saved sessions

      Save a snapshot of the night’s game counts and download a copy whenever you like.

    • Works without internet

      The manual queue runs on one device. QR check-in and the player page need a connection.

    • Private by design

      No accounts. Your session stays on your device; QR check-in shares nicknames only with people who have your session link.

    • Feedback built in

      Rate Sideout or report a bug from the app. Every message is read.
